COMPANY OVERVIEW
================
Company: RetailCo Analytics
Founded: 2018
Headquarters: Chennai, India
Business: Multi-category retail operations across South Asia

Regional Structure:
  North region: Delhi, Chandigarh, Lucknow
  South region: Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad
  East region:  Kolkata, Bhubaneswar
  West region:  Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad
  Central region: Indore, Bhopal, Nagpur

Customer Segments:
  Consumer:     Individual buyers, smaller order values
  Corporate:    Business buyers, bulk orders
  Home Office:  Remote workers, mix of products

Product Categories:
  Electronics: Highest revenue, highest return rate
  Furniture:   Highest average order value, low returns
  Office Supplies: High volume, low value
  Clothing:    Seasonal demand, moderate returns
  Food:        Perishable, consistent demand

Revenue Model:
  Primary: Direct product sales
  Growth target: 15% YoY revenue growth
