SALES POLICY DOCUMENT
======================
Version: 2.1 | Effective: January 2024

RETURN POLICY
-------------
Standard return window: 30 days from order date
Electronics:     15 days (functional defects only after 15 days)
Food items:      7 days (perishable)
Furniture:       30 days (must be unassembled)
Clothing:        30 days (unworn, tags attached)
Office Supplies: 30 days (unopened packaging)

Refund processing time: 5-7 business days

DISCOUNT POLICY
---------------
Corporate customers: 8% base discount on orders above INR 10,000
Home Office segment: 5% discount on Office Supplies
Loyalty discount:    2% for customers with 10+ orders
Seasonal:            Maximum 20% (requires manager approval)

LOW STOCK POLICY
----------------
Alert threshold:  10 units remaining
Critical:         5 units remaining
Auto-reorder:     8 units remaining
